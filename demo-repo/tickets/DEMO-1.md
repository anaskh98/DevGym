## Why

Members ask support to cancel bookings for them several times a day. They should be able to do it themselves.

## What

- A member can cancel their own booking up to 24 hours before it starts.
- A cancelled booking is removed from the room's schedule, so someone else can book the slot.
- The member gets their credits back.
- Admins can cancel bookings too.
- Let the people affected know.

## Acceptance

- Cancelling returns the updated booking.
- Existing tests keep passing, and the new behaviour is covered by tests.
